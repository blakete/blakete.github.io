* **Question every requirement:** Challenge assumptions. Know who made each requirement and why.
* **Delete parts or processes:** Remove anything unnecessary. If you never add anything back, you haven't deleted enough.
* **Simplify and optimize:** Only optimize what remains.
* **Accelerate cycle time:** Make the process faster.
* **Automate:** Automate only after the first four steps.

**Golden rule:** Never optimize something that shouldn't exist.

**Sequence:** Question → Delete → Simplify → Accelerate → Automate.

**Source:** [Starbase Tour with Elon Musk, Part 1 (Everyday Astronaut, July 2021)](https://youtu.be/t705r8ICkRw?t=809), around the 13:30 mark.
